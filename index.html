This back-test uses real historical data from FRED (US Federal Reserve) and Frankfurter.app (ECB). For each trading day in the past 60 days it reconstructs the macro conditions using actual US 10Y yields and EUR/USD rates, calculates what bias the engine would have generated that morning, then compares against what the market actually did that day.

Data sources: FRED (DGS10 — US 10-year yield) · Frankfurter.app (ECB daily FX rates). Both free and public. Fetching ~60 days takes 5–10 seconds.
